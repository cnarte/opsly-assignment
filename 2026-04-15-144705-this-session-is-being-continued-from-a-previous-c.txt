  Current GitNexus Indexing Capabilities

  GitNexus's multi-phase indexing pipeline currently supports:
  1. Structure — Folder/file relationships
  2. Parsing — Symbols (functions, classes, methods via Tree-sitter)
  3. Resolution — Imports and calls
  4. Clustering — Functional areas
  5. Processes — Execution flows
  6. Search — Hybrid BM25/semantic indexes

  Decorator extraction is NOT in this pipeline yet.

  What This Means for Your System

  Your GitNexus setup is working correctly for:
  - ✅ Finding classes, functions, methods by name
